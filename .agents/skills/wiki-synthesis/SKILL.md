# Wiki synthesis

Synthesize from `06_wiki` resources and concepts, not from recollection alone. Clearly separate evidence, inference, open questions, and recommendations. Link to the resource pages that support material conclusions.

Create synthesis pages only after user confirmation. Treat a synthesis as a dated, opinionated deliverable: do not silently rewrite its conclusion when later evidence changes. Create a new synthesis and use `supersedes` when a successor is needed.

Use frontmatter with `id`, `type: synthesis`, `title`, `status`, `created`, `updated`, `source_refs`, `relations`, and optional `supersedes`. Put substantial unanswered questions in the body rather than pretending the evidence is complete.
